# Nicola Violante

### Computer Engineering MSc Student

I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Computer Engineering at the **University of Rome Tor Vergata**, driven by a deep passion for **Cybersecurity**, **Cloud Architectures**, **Big Data Processing**, and **Low-Level Linux Kernel Engineering**.

I am particularly drawn to the engineering challenges that arise at the intersection of these fields: securing complex infrastructures, managing massive real-time and batch data streams, and optimizing performance directly at the low level of the Linux kernel.

I genuinely enjoy working close to the operating system—developing custom **Linux Kernel Modules (LKM)**, engineering low-level network defense systems using **eBPF/XDP** and **AppArmor**, and reverse-engineering Windows binaries to analyze privilege escalation vectors. Beyond the kernel space, I architect scalable **serverless AWS** environments and build end-to-end data pipelines leveraging **Apache Flink, Spark, and Kafka**. Alongside my technical work, I serve as the **Chair of the ACM Student Chapter** at my university, leading executive operations and fostering a community of computer science enthusiasts.
